Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how 204LABS handles information in connection with Number Rush, a mobile number puzzle and casual game. Number Rush does not require login, account registration, or a user profile.

Number Rush is designed to be simple. We do not operate our own app server for Number Rush, and the app does not include an account system that identifies you directly.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address through Number Rush.

Number Rush does not ask you to provide sensitive information, financial information, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or camera access.

Information Processed by Third-Party Services

Number Rush may use Google AdMob or the Google Mobile Ads SDK to support advertising. Third-party services may collect or process certain information to provide ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and improve their services.

This information may include advertising identifiers, device information, app usage information, diagnostics, and approximate location information. 204LABS does not directly collect precise GPS location through Number Rush.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices and AdMob data use at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy and data information

Advertising

Number Rush may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising identifiers and related information to deliver, limit, personalize, or measure ads, depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and applicable law.

You can manage ad personalization and advertising identifier settings through your device settings and Google settings where available.

How Information Is Used

Information processed by third-party services may be used to provide and maintain advertising, measure ad performance, detect and prevent invalid activity, comply with legal obligations, and improve service reliability.

Because Number Rush does not have user accounts, 204LABS does not use information to identify individual players through an account profile.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob as described in this Privacy Policy. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data Retention

204LABS does not maintain its own user account database for Number Rush. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to those services' own policies and legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

Number Rush is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through Number Rush.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Your Choices

You can choose not to use Number Rush. You can also manage advertising preferences, ad personalization, and advertising identifier settings through your device settings and Google settings where available.

If you contact us, you may request information about privacy-related matters connected with Number Rush.

Security

We use reasonable care when handling privacy-related requests. However, no app, device, network, or electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of Number Rush after an update means the revised policy applies.
